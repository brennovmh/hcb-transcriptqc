# Validation

- Housekeeping genes are complementary QC markers in transcriptome data.
- Housekeeping genes may be central QC criteria in targeted panels.
- `ACTB` and `GAPDH` should not be used in isolation.
- High duplication in amplicon panels should not be interpreted the same way as hybrid-capture panels.
- Chimeric reads alone do not prove fusion suitability or fusion presence.
- Negative fusion interpretation is only meaningful when RNA quality and technical controls are adequate.
